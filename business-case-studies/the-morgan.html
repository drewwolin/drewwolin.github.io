160 Morgan is the stabilized anchor of a three-phase master plan. The completion of the final phases ensures a long-term "cultural moat" for the building's valuation.

Phase II: 10 Provost

28-story luxury condo tower by Toll Brothers. Provides high-NW equity to the block.

Phase III: 151 Bay Street

Final residential tower (259 units) and the theater component.

🎭

Great Oak Theater

A 550-seat performing arts center built into the Provost Square master plan. Targeted for activation in 2026, it serves as a definitive amenity that justifies the building's 5-8% rental premium.

📈

Rockpoint Strategy

Acquired in late 2025 as a stabilized Core-Plus hold. Rockpoint's management focuses on yield optimization through tech-enabled operations and leveraging the newly matured neighborhood retail.

🚇

Quality of Life

Residents benefit from "The Morgan" lifestyle: 38th-floor rooftop views, children's playroom, and a dog spa, all within a 5-minute walk to the Grove St PATH station.

🌐

Market Resilience

As Jersey City densifies, the supply of high-rise towers with unobstructed Manhattan views is shrinking. This scarcity protects the building's cap rate through 2030.
