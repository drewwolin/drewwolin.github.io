Strategic Pivots

How the core identity and product suite evolved.

Focus: Digital Advertising Protection

Originally named White Ops, the company was built to solve a specific, massive problem: ad fraud. Brands were losing billions to bots viewing and clicking programmatic ads.

  • ✓ Key Target: DSPs, SSPs, and major advertisers.
  • ✓ Core Tech: JavaScript tags measuring human interaction in browsers.
  • ✓ Business Model: Checking ad impressions for validity before or after the bid.
🕵

The Catalyst

The discovery of the Methbot and 3ve botnets proved their tech worked at a global, state-sponsored scale, earning them FBI collaboration.

Focus: Broader Bot Management

Realizing that the same bots committing ad fraud were also taking over accounts, scraping data, and scalping inventory, the company rebranded to HUMAN.

  • ✓ Key Target: Enterprise CISOs, E-commerce, Financial Services.
  • ✓ Core Tech: "Human Verification Engine" applied to login, checkout, and search paths.
  • ✓ Brand Shift: Moving from a negative framing (White Ops/Black Ops) to a positive one (Verifying HUMANity).
👤

The Catalyst

Acquisition by Goldman Sachs Asset Management and ClearSky in 2020 provided the capital and board direction to exit the niche ad-tech market and enter mainstream cybersecurity.

Focus: AppSec & Digital Defense Platform

To compete with giants like Akamai and Cloudflare, HUMAN aggressively expanded through M&A, merging with PerimeterX to combine client-side security with bot management.

  • ✓ Key Target: Comprehensive Application Security teams.
  • ✓ New Capabilities: Client-Side Security (Magecart protection), Malvertising defense (Clean.io acquisition).
  • ✓ Scale: Verifying over 20 trillion digital interactions per week.
🔒

The Catalyst

The $100M+ growth round led by WestCap and NightDragon fueled the PerimeterX merger, instantly doubling their market footprint and engineering capacity.

Key Leaders & Eras

The executives who steered the company through its distinct phases of growth.

1

Michael Tiffany & Dan Kaminsky

The Founders (2012 - 2019)

Founded White Ops in a Brooklyn sci-fi bookstore. Kaminsky (a renowned cybersecurity legend) and Tiffany built the core philosophy of "making fraud unprofitable" by shifting defense from the network layer to the browser layer.

2

Tamer Hassan

The Scaler (2019 - 2023)

Co-founder who stepped into the CEO role. He orchestrated the massive Methbot/3ve takedowns, led the company through the Goldman Sachs acquisition, rebranded to HUMAN, and executed the PerimeterX merger.

3

Stu Solomon

The Integrator (2023 - Present)

Took over as CEO post-mergers. With a background at Recorded Future and Optiv, Solomon is tasked with consolidating the diverse product portfolio (Bot Management, AppSec, Ad Fraud) into a unified, enterprise-grade platform.

Future Outlook & Strategic Initiatives

Where HUMAN Security is heading next in the threat landscape.

1. AI-Driven Attack Mitigation

The rise of Generative AI has lowered the barrier to entry for attackers. Bots can now solve CAPTCHAs, mimic human conversational patterns, and generate fake identities. HUMAN's stated initiative is to enhance its behavioral analysis to detect AI-generated interaction patterns, fighting "bad AI with good AI."

2. The CAPTCHA-less Enterprise

Traditional friction (like image CAPTCHAs) hurts conversion rates. A core strategic push is utilizing their vast data lake (20T+ signals/week) to authenticate users passively in the background, allowing brands to drop CAPTCHAs entirely while maintaining higher security.

3. Platform Consolidation

Following the acquisitions of PerimeterX and Clean.io, HUMAN is actively working to unified their disparate agents and dashboards. The goal is a single piece of JavaScript and a single pane of glass for CISOs to manage fraud, bot mitigation, and client-side app security.
