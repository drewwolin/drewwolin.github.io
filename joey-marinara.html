The Marinara Origin Story

It started with a simple realization under a tall oak tree: life is too short for mediocre snacks. While others were playing ball, Joey was thinking about the Maillard reaction. He took his signature "Number One" attitude from the streets and brought it into the kitchen.

Today, the Chef's hat is more than a uniform—it's a crown. Joey has traded the playground for the pastry board, perfecting a secret recipe that has the neighborhood (and soon, the world) lining up before sunrise.
