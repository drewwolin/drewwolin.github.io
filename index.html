My career hasn’t just been about numbers; it's been about finding the signal in the noise; building the technical skill to reliable store, access, and wrangle data; and synthesizing data into a story and strategy anyone can understand.

Analytical curiosity has always been with me, including in high school as a basketball scout and player analyst, where I spent years breaking down player ability and projecting future success.
