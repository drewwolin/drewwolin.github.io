Got Questions?

Common Questions

All the essential details on custom specifications, resolution requests, and processing times.

How do I submit my photo files?

Once you submit your specifications through our Google Form, simply send an email containing your high-resolution PNG or JPEG file to yinzilibbs@gmail.com. Please use your full name as the email subject so we can cross-reference it with your intake form immediately!

What kind of photo works best?

High-resolution photos taken in natural daylight are highly recommended. Avoid blurry, low-light, or heavily compressed screenshots. High-contrast images with centered subjects produce incredibly rich prints on phone cases and canvas.

Is the flat rate really $25? Are there shipping fees?

Yes! Every customizable product on our site is priced at a flat rate of $25. Standard local domestic shipping is included free of charge. Applicable tax rates are processed transparently directly within the final review stage.

Can I review mockups before final production?

Absolutely. Once we receive your image and complete the mock layout matching your configuration specs, we will email you a digital preview mockup for layout approval before physically curing the ink.
