TACTICAL MATRIX

Spurs vs Knicks Matchup Dynamic

The 2026 Finals represented a profound tactical chess match. San Antonio featured generational defensive center Victor Wembanyama flanked by De'Aaron Fox and Devin Vassell. They utilized a deep physical zone to deny Jalen Brunson paint entries.

In response, Knicks coach Mike Brown deployed Karl-Anthony Towns at the five to pull Wembanyama out of the restricted area. This spacing adjustment allowed Brunson, Bridges, and Hart to exploit drive-and-kick avenues, neutralizing the Spurs' towering interior size advantages.
