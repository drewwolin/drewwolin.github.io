Our Mission

Founded by Raf, our center started with a single rescued kitten. Today, we are a thriving community hub where animal welfare meets dedicated passion. We believe every cat deserves a safe place to land and a warm lap to purr on.

  • Comprehensive medical checkups & vaccinations
  • Matching cats based on lifestyle and compatibility
  • Post-adoption support and behavior consulting
